Comparison with the sandbox behaviour in STATUS.md
--------------------------------------------------

No behavioural differences. Specifically:

  - Authentication succeeded first time against the production host with the
    production key, same shape as sandbox: POST /authenticate {"apiKey": ...}
    returning an access token.
  - Field coverage is identical to sandbox: company number and name present on
    every record (25/25 in the sample); status, next accounts made up to,
    accounts due date, last accounts made up to, confirmation statement due and
    incorporation date all absent.
  - So the Phase 2 richer company data has NOT appeared on production. The
    conclusions in README.md and STATUS.md stand unchanged:
      * the Limited Companies Tracker deadline feed still needs the manual
        portfolio export - do not point state/inform direct/ at this client;
      * the SA director / close-company check stays in the browser.

    holds 404.
